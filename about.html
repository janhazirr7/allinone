About OmniTools

Fast, free, and private online tools for everyday tasks — no accounts, no downloads, no data stored.

What OmniTools Is

OmniTools is a collection of 36 free online tools, available in 30 languages, covering everyday needs such as age and date calculations, health and fitness estimates, finance and loan math, text and writing utilities, unit conversions, and security tools like a password generator. Every tool is free to use, with no sign-up and no account required.

Our Mission

Everyday tools should be fast, completely free, and genuinely respectful of privacy. Too many simple online calculators ask you to install software, create an account, or hand over personal information. OmniTools takes the opposite approach: open a tool, use it, and leave — nothing about you is collected along the way.

How the Tools Work

All computation happens in your own web browser, on your own device. When you enter a date, an amount, or a piece of text, the calculation runs locally in the page itself. Your inputs and results are never sent to a server, never logged, and never stored by the site. That is also why the tools work instantly, with no waiting for a server round trip.

The one exception is the Currency Converter, which fetches a public exchange-rate table so it can convert between currencies. That fetch sends no personal data and no information about what you are converting.
