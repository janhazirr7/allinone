Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

At OmniTools, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This policy explains what the site does and does not collect. In short: the site has no accounts, collects no personal data, and stores nothing about you or your calculations.

1. No Accounts and No Personal Data Collected

OmniTools does not offer accounts, sign-ups, or user profiles, and it does not ask you to provide personal information in order to use the tools. The site itself does not collect, store, or share your name, contact details, or any other personal data. If you choose to type something into a tool — for example, a date, an amount, or a piece of text — that input stays in your browser and is never sent to or stored by the site.

2. Calculations Run in Your Browser

All computation happens in your own web browser, on your own device. Whether you are calculating an age, a loan payment, a BMI value, or generating a password, the work is done locally in the page. Your inputs and results are never transmitted to a server, logged, or stored by the site.

3. Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. Because the site is hosted as static pages, the hosting provider may handle requests in the ordinary way any web host does, but OmniTools does not use cookies of its own to track you, remember you, or build a profile of your visits.

4. Currency Converter and the Exchange-Rate Table

The Currency Converter is the one tool that reaches outside your browser: it fetches a public exchange-rate table so it can convert between currencies. That fetch sends no personal data and no information about what you are converting. No other tool makes an outside request as part of its calculation.

5. Third-Party Links

The site may link to third-party websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and OmniTools has no control over what they collect. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit through a link from OmniTools.

6. Advertising in the Future

The site currently carries no advertising network. If advertising is added in the future, this policy will be updated before or at the same time to describe what any advertising provider collects and how you can control it.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time, and the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will change when it is. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the Contact page.
