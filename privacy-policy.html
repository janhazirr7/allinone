Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

At OmniTools (accessible from https://omnitools.pages.dev/), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by OmniTools and how we utilize it.

1. Client-Side Execution Guarantee

OmniTools is built on a client-side architecture. Calculations performed in our tools (including the Age Calculator, BMI Tracker, Loan Calculator, Password Generator, etc.) are executed entirely on your personal web browser using local JavaScript. We do not transmit, log, or store your calculation inputs or generated results on any remote server or database.

2. Log Files

Like standard hosting services (including GitHub Pages and Cloudflare), standard log files may register visitors when they access websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable and are used solely for site security and performance monitoring.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies & Google AdSense

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on OmniTools. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit. Note that OmniTools has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California (CCPA), you are entitled to certain privacy rights under the General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act. Because OmniTools does not collect personal identity information, accounts, or user profiles, your risk of personal data exposure is inherently zero.

5. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through our Contact Page.
