How to Use the Salary to Hourly Calculator

Job offers are usually quoted as annual salaries, while freelance work, part-time jobs, and overtime are quoted by the hour, and comparing the two in your head is surprisingly easy to get wrong. This calculator converts in both directions of scale: enter an annual or monthly salary plus your weekly hours, and it shows the equivalent hourly, weekly, monthly, and annual pay, so you can compare any offer against any other on equal terms.

  1. Enter your salary amountType the gross salary before taxes, for example 52000 for a fifty-two-thousand-dollar annual salary.
  2. Choose the salary periodSelect Annual salary if the amount is yearly, or Monthly salary if you entered a monthly figure. The calculator annualizes a monthly salary by multiplying by 12.
  3. Enter your hours per weekType your usual weekly hours, for example 40 for standard full-time work, or 37.5 or 20 for other schedules.
  4. CalculatePress Calculate Hourly Rate to see your hourly rate plus the weekly, monthly, and annual equivalents.

The Salary Conversion Formulas Explained

The conversion rests on one simple convention: a year has 52 weeks. Everything else follows from that single assumption, which is the standard used by payroll departments and job boards, even though real years have slightly more than 52 weeks and paid holidays complicate the picture.

Hourly = Annual salary / (52 × hours per week)
Where:
• Weekly pay = Annual salary / 52
• Monthly pay = Annual salary / 12
• Annual salary = Monthly salary × 12 (when monthly is selected)
• All results are gross figures, before income tax, social contributions, and other deductions.

Hours matter just as much as salary. Two jobs both paying $52,000 are not equal if one demands 40 hours a week and the other demands 50: the first pays $25.00 an hour, while the second pays only $20.00 an hour for the same annual sum. That is why hourly conversion is the fairest way to compare offers, especially when one role includes regular overtime or a longer working week.

Worked Example: $52,000 a Year at 40 Hours a Week

Take an annual salary of $52,000 with a 40-hour week. Weekly pay is $52,000 / 52, which equals $1,000. Monthly pay is $52,000 / 12, which equals $4,333.33. The hourly rate is $52,000 / (52 × 40), which is $52,000 / 2,080, giving exactly $25.00 per hour. These are clean numbers because $52,000 was chosen to divide neatly, but the calculator handles any salary and any hours, including part-time schedules such as 20 hours, where the same $52,000 annual figure would mean $50.00 an hour.

A monthly example shows the other direction. A monthly salary of $4,000 annualizes to $48,000, which gives weekly pay of $923.08 and, at 40 hours a week, an hourly rate of $23.08. Comparing that against a freelance offer of $25 an hour is now straightforward: the freelance rate is higher per hour, but the salaried role may include paid holidays, sick pay, pension contributions, and job security that freelance work does not, so the hourly figure is only one part of the decision.

1. Compare offers fairly

Convert every offer to an hourly rate at its real weekly hours before deciding. A higher annual salary can hide a lower hourly rate when the working week is longer.

2. Price freelance work

Freelancers should charge more per hour than the equivalent salary rate, because they pay their own taxes, holidays, and downtime. A common starting point is 1.5 to 2 times the salaried hourly equivalent.

3. Remember it is gross pay

These results are before tax. Your take-home pay will be lower after income tax and contributions, and the exact deduction depends on your country, tax band, and personal circumstances.

One limitation to keep in mind: the calculator assumes you are paid for all 52 weeks, including holidays. If your contract gives unpaid leave, or if overtime is paid at a higher rate, your effective hourly value of worked hours will differ slightly. For offer comparison and budgeting, however, the 52-week convention is the standard everyone uses, and it keeps comparisons honest.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How do I convert an annual salary to an hourly rate?
Divide the annual salary by 52 weeks and then by your hours per week: hourly = annual / (52 × hours). For example, $52,000 a year at 40 hours a week gives $52,000 / 2,080 = $25.00 per hour.
Why is 52 weeks used in the calculation?
Fifty-two weeks is the standard payroll convention for a year, used by employers and job boards worldwide. Real calendar years have 52 weeks plus one or two days, and paid holidays vary, but 52 weeks keeps every offer comparable on the same basis.
How do I convert a monthly salary to hourly pay?
First annualize it by multiplying by 12, then apply the same formula. A monthly salary of $4,000 becomes $48,000 a year, which at 40 hours a week gives $23.08 per hour. This calculator does both steps for you when you select Monthly salary.
Are the results gross or net pay?
All results are gross pay, before income tax, social security, pension contributions, and other deductions. Your take-home pay will be lower, and the exact amount depends on your country's tax system and your personal situation.
Is my salary data saved or sent anywhere?
No. OmniTools runs entirely in your browser. Your salary and hours are never sent to a server, stored in a database, or shared with anyone.
