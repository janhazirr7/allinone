What Is Lorem Ipsum and Where Did It Come From?

Lorem Ipsum is not random gibberish. It comes from a real Latin work, "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (On the Ends of Good and Evil), written by the Roman statesman and philosopher Cicero in 45 BC. The famous opening, "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit", is a scrambled version of a passage from that book, in which Cicero discusses the nature of pleasure and pain. Over the centuries, typesetters kept the text because its mix of word lengths looks like natural English or Latin prose, even though readers cannot actually read it as meaningful sentences.

The text became a printing industry standard in the 1500s, when an unknown printer scrambled Cicero's words into a specimen book of typefaces. It survived into the digital age with desktop publishing software in the 1980s, and today it is built into design tools everywhere. The reason it endures is simple: when a client or reviewer looks at a mockup, readable text pulls their attention to the words themselves. They start proofreading instead of judging spacing, hierarchy, and visual balance. Placeholder text removes that distraction, so the design itself gets the feedback.

That is also why good Lorem Ipsum is varied. A single sentence repeated twenty times looks obviously fake and breaks the illusion of a finished page. A proper bank of sentences, with different lengths and rhythms, mimics the texture of real writing: short punchy sentences mixed with longer flowing ones. This generator uses fourteen classic sentences drawn from the traditional Lorem Ipsum passage, rotated so each paragraph has a different combination.

When Should You Use Placeholder Text?

Placeholder text is useful far beyond graphic design. Web developers use it to test how a page behaves when content is long or short, and whether text wraps or overflows on small phone screens. Content writers use it to reserve space in an article outline while research is still underway. Teachers use it to create formatting exercises without giving away answers, and app developers use it to test database fields, search results, and user profiles before real user data exists.

There is one important rule: never ship placeholder text to real users. It is a design tool, not final content. Before a website, app, or document goes live, every block of Lorem Ipsum should be replaced with real, proofread copy in the language your audience speaks. Search engines also penalize pages that publish filler text as if it were content, so treat generation as a drafting step, never the finished product.

Tips for Realistic Mockups

First, match the length of the real content you expect. If a product description will be about fifty words, generating five hundred words will make the layout look cramped and give a false impression of the final design. Second, mix heading sizes with body text rather than filling every block identically, because real pages have a clear hierarchy. Third, test your layout with both a short and a long version of the text: a design that only works with exactly three lines of text will break the moment a real user writes four. Finally, remember that this generator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you generate is sent to a server or stored anywhere, so you can use it freely for commercial mockups, client presentations, and classroom work without signing up or paying anything.
