How to Use the Time Zone Converter

  1. Enter the source timeType the date and time exactly as it appears in the source city, for example 3 p.m. in New York on a meeting invite.
  2. Choose both zonesPick the source zone on the left and the target zone on the right from the list of 30 major world cities.
  3. Press Convert TimeThe tool shows the same moment in the target city, both UTC offsets, the hour difference, and whether the date changes.
  4. Swap if neededThe Swap Zones button reverses the conversion instantly, which is handy when you need the time both ways.

How Time Zone Conversion Really Works

Every time zone is an offset from Coordinated Universal Time, known as UTC. London in winter is UTC plus zero, Athens is UTC plus two in winter and plus three in summer, and Tokyo is always UTC plus nine. The tool does not simply add a fixed number of hours, because many zones change their offset during the year for daylight saving time. Instead it uses your browser built-in international time library, which knows the official rules for each zone on your chosen date. It first works out what instant your source wall-clock time represents, then asks what wall-clock time that same instant shows in the target zone. That two-step method is why a New York to London conversion in January gives a five-hour gap, while the same conversion in July gives a five-hour gap too but with different offsets, and why a New York to Sydney conversion can land on a different calendar day.

Why the Date Sometimes Changes

  • A late evening meeting in Los Angeles is already early morning the next day in London, Tokyo and Sydney, so inviting people without checking can mean someone joins at 3 a.m.
  • A morning call from Europe to Australia may fall on the previous evening in Sydney if you count backwards, which matters for deadlines.
  • Flights that appear to arrive before they depart, such as a flight leaving Tokyo and landing in Los Angeles earlier the same day, are simply crossing the International Date Line.
  • Sports and live events: a match at 8 p.m. in Madrid is at 2 p.m. in New York and 6 a.m. the next day in Sydney, so fans in different continents watch on different days.

Planning Meetings Across Zones

The kindest meeting time is one where nobody has to join in the middle of the night. For a team spread across Europe, Asia and the Americas, late afternoon in Europe is often morning in the Americas and evening in Asia, which is usually the best compromise. Always write the time zone next to the time in an invitation, for example 15:00 Athens time, rather than just 15:00, because a bare time is meaningless across borders. Send calendar invites instead of plain text when you can, because calendars convert automatically, but double-check the converted time with this tool before sending anything critical. Finally, remember that daylight saving starts and ends on different dates in the United States, Europe and Australia, so a meeting time that worked in March may shift by an hour in April even if nobody changed it.

Privacy and Limits

This converter runs entirely in your browser using the standard time-zone data built into your device, so no time, city or meeting detail is ever uploaded or stored. The list covers 30 major world zones, which handles almost every business, travel and family need, but it does not list every small region; if your city is missing, pick the nearest major city in the same zone, as it will usually share the same offset and daylight saving rules. The tool shows whole-hour and half-hour offsets correctly, including India at plus five and a half hours and Nepal-style zones are not in the list, so for unusual half-hour or 45-minute zones, check the official offset for that city before relying on the result for a critical flight or exam.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Why is the gap between two cities not always the same?
Because daylight saving time starts and ends on different dates in different countries. The tool uses the official rules for your chosen date, so it gives the correct gap for that date, not a fixed yearly average.
What does UTC+2 mean?
It means local time is two hours ahead of Coordinated Universal Time. When it is 12:00 UTC, it is 14:00 in a UTC+2 zone, before any daylight saving adjustment already included in the offset shown.
Can the converted time fall on a different day?
Yes, very often. Evening in the Americas is already the next day in Europe and Asia, and morning in Asia can be the previous evening in the Americas. The tool shows the day shift clearly.
Is my meeting time sent to a server?
No. The conversion runs in your browser with built-in time-zone data, and nothing you enter leaves your device.
What if my city is not in the list?
Pick the nearest major city in the same time zone. Most cities in one zone share the same offset and daylight saving rules, so the result will match for everyday planning.
